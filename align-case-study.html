Independent concept / Fictional brand

Align: the thinking behind the page.

Help an independent founder decide whether coaching fits, then preview an application.

This is a self-initiated design exercise. No client, testimonial, live conversion result or commercial relationship is claimed.

Explore the interactive Align demo ↗

The brief and audience

Independent founders refining an offer or deciding what to focus on next. The visitor needs to understand fit before sharing details.

Funnel map

Offer → for / not for → four-step application → review → mock booking.

Why these sections exist

A fit section sets expectations before the form. One question group appears at a time, with progress and a back button. A review step makes the final action deliberate. The booking screen is explicitly simulated.

From structure to visual direction

The wireframe starts with the visitor’s question, followed by the information needed to take the next step. The final concept adds a distinct visual language while keeping the action in the same hierarchy.

Wireframe

PropositionOffer context / previewDecision supportPrimary action

Final direction

Warm ivory, forest green and a spacious serif heading support a reflective coaching conversation. The progress indicator and review turn the form into a guided sequence.

Desktop and mobile views

The layout stacks on narrow screens. Align and Forma retain a mobile action bar; Fieldnotes keeps the short form in the main reading flow. Tap a preview to enlarge it.

What I would A/B test

A specific outcome headline versus a broader clarity headline; starting with the business question versus contact details. Compare application completion and qualified enquiries, without assuming either version wins.

These are hypotheses, not reported findings. A real test would need a defined audience, sufficient traffic, a primary metric and a quality check on the leads or purchases.

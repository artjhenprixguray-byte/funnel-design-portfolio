Independent concept / Fictional brand

Fieldnotes: the thinking behind the page.

Make the value of a planning guide clear before an email-only opt-in preview.

This is a self-initiated design exercise. No client, testimonial, live conversion result or commercial relationship is claimed.

Explore the interactive Fieldnotes demo ↗

The brief and audience

Independent business owners with competing priorities who want a simple way to choose their next focus.

Funnel map

Guide promise + preview → benefits → email-only form → thank-you → sample planning prompt.

Why these sections exist

One email field reduces the number of decisions required. The guide mockup makes the proposed resource visible. The confirmation gives one next step and states that no email was sent. A small pressed-button interaction provides feedback without heavy animation.

From structure to visual direction

The wireframe starts with the visitor’s question, followed by the information needed to take the next step. The final concept adds a distinct visual language while keeping the action in the same hierarchy.

Wireframe

PropositionOffer context / previewDecision supportPrimary action

Final direction

Paper tones, a serif heading and a CSS guide cover make the resource tangible. The email field stays close to the benefits, with no competing signup action.

Desktop and mobile views

The layout stacks on narrow screens. Align and Forma retain a mobile action bar; Fieldnotes keeps the short form in the main reading flow. Tap a preview to enlarge it.

What I would A/B test

A guide-focused headline versus a priority-focused headline; benefits above versus beside the form. Compare valid opt-ins and subsequent resource use in a real launch.

These are hypotheses, not reported findings. A real test would need a defined audience, sufficient traffic, a primary metric and a quality check on the leads or purchases.

AlignCoaching application funnel

A clearer path to a discovery call

An application funnel that introduces the offer, answers key questions and guides visitors toward a conversation.

Read the Align design decisions
Goal
Help an independent founder decide whether coaching fits, then preview an application.
Fictional audience
Independent founders refining an offer or deciding what to focus on next. The visitor needs to understand fit before sharing details.
Funnel flow
Offer → for / not for → four-step application → review → mock booking.
Key decisions
A fit section sets expectations before the form. One question group appears at a time, with progress and a back button. A review step makes the final action deliberate. The booking screen is explicitly simulated.
What I would A/B test
A specific outcome headline versus a broader clarity headline; starting with the business question versus contact details. Compare application completion and qualified enquiries, without assuming either version wins.

FormaDigital course sales page

From curiosity to consideration

A course sales page built around a tangible curriculum and a clearly explained offer.

Read the Forma design decisions
Goal
Help a creator understand a brand foundations course before previewing checkout.
Fictional audience
Independent creators who have an idea but need a more consistent visual and verbal identity.
Funnel flow
Course proposition → interior preview → expandable curriculum → offer → FAQs → mock checkout.
Key decisions
A CSS course interior makes the product tangible without stock photos. Expandable modules let visitors choose their level of detail. The offer groups inclusions and unconfirmed commercial terms in one place. Checkout never asks for payment.
What I would A/B test
The course interior versus the curriculum as the first supporting section; “Preview checkout” versus “Explore the course”. Measure offer engagement and checkout intent once a real product and valid tracking exist.

FieldnotesFree guide opt-in

A useful first impression

A focused opt-in journey that explains the value of a free guide before asking for an email.

Read the Fieldnotes design decisions
Goal
Make the value of a planning guide clear before an email-only opt-in preview.
Fictional audience
Independent business owners with competing priorities who want a simple way to choose their next focus.
Funnel flow
Guide promise + preview → benefits → email-only form → thank-you → sample planning prompt.
Key decisions
One email field reduces the number of decisions required. The guide mockup makes the proposed resource visible. The confirmation gives one next step and states that no email was sent. A small pressed-button interaction provides feedback without heavy animation.
What I would A/B test
A guide-focused headline versus a priority-focused headline; benefits above versus beside the form. Compare valid opt-ins and subsequent resource use in a real launch.

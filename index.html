How do revisions work?

The proposed scope includes two consolidated revision rounds. This is a draft term; the final agreement will confirm the number of rounds and what counts as a scope change.

Can you help with the copy?

I can shape the page structure and refine headlines and calls to action. You provide the facts behind the offer. Full copywriting can be discussed and scoped separately.

Which platforms can you work with?

Figma for design and HTML/CSS/JS for responsive static builds. For other platforms, I can prepare a design handoff; platform implementation needs to be agreed separately.

How does payment work?

Payment schedule, currency, and method are setup slots. You’ll receive these terms with the written scope before starting; this website does not take payment.

When could my project be ready?

The draft estimates above begin after the brief and content arrive. Page count, integrations, availability, and feedback timing affect the schedule. We’ll confirm a start and delivery date together.

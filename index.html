02 / THE APPROACH

A funnel is a conversation.
Let’s make it a good one.

Every section should earn its place.
Here’s how I approach the work.

01 — DISCOVER

Start with the person.

Understand your audience, your offer, and the action you want visitors to take.

02 — MAP

Find the right flow.

Arrange the message around the questions, hesitations, and decisions that matter.

03 — DESIGN

Bring it into focus.

Use visual hierarchy, considered spacing, and clear calls to action to guide attention.

04 — REFINE

Check every step.

Review the experience on mobile and desktop, refine the details, and prepare the handoff.

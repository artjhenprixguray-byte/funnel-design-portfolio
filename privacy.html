A little clarity about privacy.

Project enquiries

In the current setup, form details stay in your browser until you choose to open and send an email. Preparing a draft does not submit an enquiry. Your email provider handles the email you send.

Checklist signup

Signup is currently unavailable. No details are collected and there is no live PDF download. When a Formspree endpoint is connected, the form will identify Formspree as the processor before submission. A checklist request does not imply consent to a newsletter.

Optional form service

If direct sending is enabled, name, email, project choice and message are sent to Formspree for delivery to Art. Only share what is needed for the enquiry. The form’s message states which mode is active.

Analytics and hosting

No analytics tracker is active in this version. There is a setup slot for Plausible. If enabled, named CTA events will contain no form values or email addresses. GitHub Pages hosts the site. Optional Google Fonts requests are made to Google to display the typefaces.

Your questions

Contact artjhenprix.guray@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests about information you have shared. Retention terms will be finalized before direct form collection is enabled.
